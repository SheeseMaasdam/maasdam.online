Hello! This is the first blog post of the **maasdam.online** project.

## What is maasdam.online?

It's a gaming community by Vladimir "Syr" Maasdam. Several servers are running here for multiplayer fun:

- ⛏️ **Minecraft** — survival mode with custom plugins at `mc.maasdam.online`
- 🌍 **Rimworld** — multiplayer storytelling & survival at `rim.maasdam.online`

## How to start playing?

All the game files you need are in our **Discord server**. Come join us — you'll also find the latest news and community chat there.

## What's next?

The project is actively developing. Keep an eye on the blog — here you'll find:

- Server news and updates
- Patch notes
- Event announcements

See you on the servers! 👾
